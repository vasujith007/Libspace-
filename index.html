Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check in with my QR Pass?

Open your Digital Pass on your phone or printout. When you arrive at your seat within 1 hour of start time, click "Check In" or scan at the kiosk.

Can I extend my reservation?

Yes! If the desk is not reserved by another student right after your slot, you can click "⏱️ +30m Extend" from your active banner or bookings list.

What are the quiet zone guidelines?

Floor 1 Quiet Zone requires silent study and headphones for audio. Group discussions are welcomed on Floor 2.
